Questions

Good to know

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Workouts are generated and run entirely on your device.

Can I hear cues while music plays?

Yes. Beeps and spoken cues briefly lower the volume of your music or podcast instead of stopping it. Voice, beeps and haptics can each be switched off.

Does the timer keep going with the screen locked?

Yes. WFH keeps timing and playing cues in the background while a workout is running.

What if I don't own the equipment in a shared workout?

WFH flags the exercises you can't do with your kit and lets you swap them before you start.

Which languages are supported?

English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Magyar, Polski and Português (Brasil), including the whole exercise library.
