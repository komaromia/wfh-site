Home workouts built for the gear you own

Your equipment. Your time. A fresh workout with one tap. KWorkout guides every set, interval and rest with timers and voice cues. Private, offline and ad-free.

Download on the App Store
Private by design. Workouts are generated on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.
  • Tell it your kit once: dumbbell weights, bands, bars, available space and limitations
  • Pick a duration, goal and focus, then generate, swap or reroll any exercise
  • Use the hands-free player for sets, intervals, EMOM, Tabata and AMRAP, with spoken cues and haptics
  • Rate and favourite workouts, replay a session exactly or ask for a similar one
  • Share a workout as a .kworkout file and open it on a friend’s phone
