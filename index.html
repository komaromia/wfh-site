KWorkout

The gear you own. The time you have. Go.

KWorkout builds a fresh home workout from your equipment and the minutes you have, then runs it for you with big timers, rep counters and voice cues.

Private by design. Workouts are generated on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.
  • Tell it your kit once: dumbbell weights, bands, bars, space and limitations
  • Pick time, goal and focus, then generate, swap or reroll any exercise
  • A hands-free player for sets, intervals, EMOM, Tabata and AMRAP, with spoken cues and haptics that work with the screen locked and music playing
  • History with ratings and favourites; replay a session exactly or ask for a similar one
  • Share a workout as a .kworkout file and open it on a friend's phone
