WFH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

WFH is a home-workout generator and timer. It was built so that your training data stays yours. In short: WFH does not collect, see, sell or share your data. There is no account and no server operated by the developer.

What the app stores

Your equipment kits, limitations, custom exercises, generated workouts, workout history, ratings, favourites and settings are stored on your device in WFH's private app container.

Your device backup may include WFH data according to your iCloud Backup or computer-backup settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in WFH, finished workouts (type, start and end time, and distance for runs and walks) are saved to Apple Health, and WFH reads heart-rate samples from Apple Health for each workout's time window to show your average and maximum. Health data is read and written only on your device, is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is never used for advertising. You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › WFH.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, WFH syncs your profile settings, equipment kits, custom exercises, saved workouts and workout history between your own devices using your private iCloud (CloudKit) database. Apple stores this data in your iCloud account; the developer cannot access it. Sounds, haptics and appearance settings stay on each device. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device.

Workout generation

Workouts are generated on your device, using the rules built into the app. Your equipment, goals and history are not sent to the developer or to any cloud AI service.

Audio, haptics and the background

WFH plays beeps and spoken cues using the system speech synthesiser on your device, mixing with or lowering your music rather than stopping it. It runs in the background during a workout only so timers and cues stay accurate. The app does not use the microphone or record audio.

Sharing and importing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to. When you share a workout as a .kworkout file, it contains the plan only (title, exercises, prescriptions, notes, and an author name if you set one), not your history, ratings or health data. The app or person you send it to handles it under their own terms and privacy policy. Files you import are checked before use and are never executed.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

WFH contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and shows no ads. This website uses no cookies, analytics or external fonts. Apple may provide aggregated App Store performance information and opt-in diagnostic reports under Apple's privacy terms; these do not contain your workout data.

Deleting your data

You can delete workouts, exercises and history inside the app; with iCloud sync on, deletions also remove the data from your iCloud and your other devices. Deleting WFH removes its local data from the device; synced data stays in your iCloud until you delete it in the app or under Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Copies you exported or included in a device backup are controlled separately.

Children

WFH does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes
