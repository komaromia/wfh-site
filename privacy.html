Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

WFH is a home-workout generator and timer built so your training data stays yours. WFH does not collect, view, sell or share your data. There is no account and no developer-operated server.

What the app stores

Your equipment kits, limitations, custom exercises, generated workouts, workout history, ratings, favourites and settings are stored on your device in WFH’s private app container. Your device backup may include this data according to your iCloud Backup or computer-backup settings; Apple handles those backups under its Privacy Policy.

iCloud sync

WFH does not currently sync your data. If sync is added in the future, it will use your private iCloud (CloudKit) storage, which the developer cannot access, and this policy will be updated first.

Workout generation

Workouts are generated on your device using rules built into the app. Your equipment, goals and history are not sent to the developer or any cloud AI service.

Audio, haptics and background use

WFH plays beeps and spoken cues with the system speech synthesiser and runs in the background during a workout only to keep timers and cues accurate. It does not use the microphone or record audio.

Sharing and importing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. A .kworkout file contains the plan only—such as its title, exercises, prescriptions, notes and an author name if you set one—not your history, ratings or health data. Imported files are checked before use and are never executed.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

WFH contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and shows no ads. This website uses no cookies, analytics or external fonts. Apple may provide aggregated App Store performance information and opt-in diagnostic reports under Apple’s privacy terms; these do not contain your workout data.

Deleting your data

You can delete workouts, exercises and history inside the app. Deleting WFH removes its local data from the device. Exported copies and device backups are controlled separately.

Children

WFH does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
