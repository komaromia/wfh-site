Help & Support

Something not working, or an idea for KWorkout? Email komaromia94@gmail.com. For a problem, include your device model, iOS version and the step where it occurred.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in KWorkout's private app container. KWorkout has no server and no account, and the developer cannot see your data. Sync between devices is not offered yet. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Workouts are generated and run entirely on your device.

Can I hear cues while my music plays?

Yes. Beeps and spoken cues lower the volume of your music or podcast briefly instead of stopping it. Voice, beeps and haptics can each be turned off in Settings.

Does the timer keep running with the screen locked?

Yes. KWorkout keeps timing and playing cues in the background while a workout is running, .

How do I share a workout?

Open a workout and choose Share to create a .kworkout file. It contains the plan only (exercises, prescriptions, title and notes), not your history or ratings. The recipient opens it from Messages, AirDrop, Mail or Files and can substitute any equipment they do not own.

How do I delete my data?

Delete workouts and exercises inside the app. Deleting KWorkout removes its data from the device.

Exercise safety

KWorkout gives general fitness suggestions, not medical advice. See the Terms.
